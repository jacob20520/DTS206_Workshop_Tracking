# Email 3: CTO → NHS Trust Contacts (T+48 Hours)

**Subject:** SECURITY NOTICE: Ransomware Incident Affecting MediCore Patient Data Availability

**Inform:** A ransomware incident has affected approximately 47,000 patient clinical records under our management, which includes data subjects belonging to your specific NHS Trust. Affected categories include patient clinical notes, diagnoses, and NHS numbers.

**Confirm:** All affected production systems have been completely isolated from internal and external networks. A formal Article 33 data breach notification has been submitted to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Core clinical records systems will remain entirely unavailable until full verification and recovery protocols are complete.

**Estimated Restoration Timeline:** Full system restoration, data integrity checks, and validation testing are estimated to take between 48 to 72 hours. Systems will not be reconnected until verified clean.

**Advise:** Please activate your local business continuity policies immediately. For urgent clinical decisions requiring access to the currently unavailable records, please utilize paper records or alternative offline lookup procedures as per your Trust's contingency agreement.

**Next Update:** The next technical status update will be provided within 24 hours.
